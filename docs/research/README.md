# Research and retained evidence

Start with the governing specification linked under each topic. Research explains
why a choice was made or where evidence remains limited; it does not override
current contracts. Dates, models, trial counts, failures and observation gaps
belong to their recorded conditions. Older ownership and routing proposals are
historical even when their original text says “current” or “proposed.”
