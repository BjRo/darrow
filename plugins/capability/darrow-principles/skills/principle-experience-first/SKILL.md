# Apply experience first

Consider the experience of the end user, caller and maintainer when choosing
usability, API shape, feedback or implementation structure. Use the task's
requirements, evidence and stated preferences to judge the tradeoff.

Explain product changes as recommendations unless the caller delegated that
decision. A technically appealing experience improvement does not grant scope
or product authority.

Complete when: the choice serves the relevant user, caller or maintainer
within accepted requirements and delegated decision authority.
