# Apply subtract before you add

Before extending, refactoring or rewriting a relevant area, identify dead
weight that obstructs the simpler solution: obsolete branches, unused concepts
or duplicated decisions.

Remove the relevant dead weight within the task's authority before building
on that simpler base. Preserve required behavior and compatibility; an adjacent
cleanup opportunity is not permission to expand the task.

Complete when: the needed addition rests on the simplest relevant base and
required behavior still holds.
