# Apply exhaust the design space

For a consequential architectural or interaction choice without clear
precedent, compare two or three structurally different alternatives before
committing to one. Make the important tradeoffs visible in proportion to
their consequences.

Use sketches, types, examples or a small experiment when they resolve the
uncertainty. Full implementations, additional agents or particular models
are not required. Keep tools and delegation conditional on their benefit,
available host permissions and the owner's execution contract.

Complete when: the material structural alternatives and reasons for the chosen
one are clear at a cost proportionate to the decision.
