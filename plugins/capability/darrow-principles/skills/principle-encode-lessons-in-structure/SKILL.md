# Apply encode lessons in structure

When the same correction or instruction recurs, prefer enforceable structure
when possible: a suitable type, boundary, default, check or other durable
constraint that prevents the mistake.

Keep judgment guidance when the lesson cannot be enforced structurally.
Choose a proportionate change rather than building infrastructure merely to
encode a one-off preference.

Complete when: recurring enforceable lessons live in suitable structure and
the remaining judgment still has clear guidance.
